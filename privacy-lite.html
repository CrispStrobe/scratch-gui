Brickwright Lite Privacy Policy

Updated September 2026

Brickwright Lite respects your privacy. For transparency, this page explains what data is accessed and how it is handled.

No User Accounts or Usernames

Brickwright Lite does not use, collect, or store any usernames, aliases, or accounts. All of your work is completely local, and we do not ask for any personal information.

Cloud Services and Extensions

Unlike standard Scratch or TurboWarp, Brickwright Lite is designed primarily for offline and local hardware control (via Bluetooth/USB). It does not automatically upload your projects or data to any cloud service.

If you use extensions that require external APIs (e.g., text-to-speech, translation), those extensions may communicate with those third-party services, but this is initiated entirely by your own projects. Brickwright Lite itself does not log or proxy this data.

Local Hardware Control (Bluetooth/USB)

Brickwright Lite uses Bluetooth and USB permissions exclusively to connect directly to your programmable LEGO hardware. We do not track, log, or upload information about your connected devices.

Settings and Local Storage

Brickwright Lite stores your editor settings (such as theme preferences and editor state) locally on your device.

Analytics

We do not collect any telemetry, usage analytics, or crash reports automatically. Everything stays on your device.

Contact

Any concerns related to privacy or any other matter should be sent to: contact@turbowarp.org
